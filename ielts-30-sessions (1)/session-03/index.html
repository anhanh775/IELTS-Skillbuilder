IELTS Speaking Part 1: Education

For each question: read the model, learn the language, then plan what you will say.

Open a question. Follow the 4 steps:

1. Model answer (A answer, R reason, E example) · 2. Vocabulary (words, collocations, phrases, idioms) · 3. Sentence structures · 4. Plan your ideas, then speak for 30 seconds.

Tips and practice

Answer in 3 parts

Part 1 has short questions about your life. Give 2 or 3 sentences, about 15 to 25 seconds. Do not answer with only “Yes” or “No”.

Answer directly → Reason (because …) → Example or detail (For example, … / Sometimes …)

QuestionToo shortBetter
Do you like your school?Yes, I do.Yes, I do. My school is small, so teachers know every student. Also, my classmates often help me.

Common mistakes

WrongCorrect
I am agree.I agree.
I very like maths.I really like maths.
My school have many teachers.My school has many teachers.
I prefer study alone.I prefer studying alone.
Yes, I do. (then silence)Yes, I do. I like it because …
